# Finance bug register

Confirmed by code inspection and regression tests on 16 October 2026. These entries describe defects in the existing invoice/payment workflows; they do not imply that the wider accounting integration is complete. See [FINANCE_ACCOUNTING_STATUS.md](FINANCE_ACCOUNTING_STATUS.md) for the remaining work.

| ID | Severity | Module | Confirmed root cause and impact | Fix | Regression test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FIN-01 | High | Invoice creation | `count() + 1` generated the invoice number. Soft deletion, earlier-year invoices, and concurrent requests could generate an existing number, so invoice creation failed on the unique constraint. | Allocate the next number for the current year under a PostgreSQL advisory transaction lock, using the maximum existing number. | `invoice.service.test.ts` checks allocation; `payment.integration.test.ts` creates two invoices concurrently in PostgreSQL. |
| FIN-02 | High | Payment idempotency | An existing global `Idempotency-Key` returned its payment record before checking the requested invoice, amount, method, or client scope. Reusing a key could disclose another invoice's payment and silently accept a different request. | Check invoice ownership before replay for EVC; manual payments check scope inside the transaction. Reject mismatched replay parameters with HTTP 409. | `payment.service.test.ts` checks mismatched invoice replay. |
| FIN-03 | Critical | Manual payment settlement | The invoice balance was read before the payment transaction. Concurrent payments could both pass the remaining-balance check and overwrite `paidAmount`, creating payment records that do not reconcile with the invoice. | Lock the invoice row and read its current balance within the same payment transaction before creating a payment. | `payment.service.test.ts` exercises the locked transaction path; `payment.integration.test.ts` submits two simultaneous payments against one invoice and verifies exactly one settlement. |
| FIN-04 | Critical | EVC webhook settlement | Webhook retries and concurrent manual payments read `PENDING` and invoice balance outside the transaction, allowing duplicate or stale settlement decisions. | Lock the invoice row, reload the payment and balance, and update both in one transaction. | `payment.service.test.ts` exercises the webhook transaction; `payment.integration.test.ts` sends two simultaneous callbacks and verifies a single invoice allocation. |
| FIN-05 | High | Invoice void | `PARTIAL` invoices were voidable even when money had already been recorded; a pending EVC charge could also complete after a void. | Block void when `paidAmount > 0` or a pending payment exists, lock the invoice during void, and lock it while creating pending EVC payments. | `invoice.service.test.ts` checks partial and pending payment rejection. |
| FIN-06 | Critical | Pending payment reservation | New manual or EVC charges checked `paidAmount` but ignored outstanding `PENDING` charges. The provider could collect more than the invoice's remaining balance before callbacks arrived. | Under the invoice lock, include the sum of pending payments in the available-balance check before recording or initiating a charge. | `payment.service.test.ts` and `payment.integration.test.ts` reject charges that exceed unreserved balance. |
| FIN-07 | Critical | Payment method routing | The manual gateway handled every method except EVC, including Stripe and eDahab, immediately recording them as `COMPLETED` without a provider charge or verified settlement. | Accept only bank transfer and manual methods on the manual endpoint; return HTTP 400 for unsupported gateways. | `payment.service.test.ts` rejects Stripe and eDahab without creating a payment. |

The PostgreSQL integration test passed against a local disposable `somwave_payment_test` database. It does not exercise a live payment provider. Gateway settlement, ledger posting, and invoice/payment reconciliation remain separate acceptance gaps.
